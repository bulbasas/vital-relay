# Security policy

The relay forwards health data, so security reports are very welcome.

**Report privately** through GitHub: *Security → Report a vulnerability* on this repository. Please do not open a
public issue for anything that could expose user data or let someone impersonate a phone or an AI connection.

In scope: authentication of phones and AI connections, anything that could make the relay persist or log health data or
connection secrets, request routing between devices, denial of service against a single user's phone.

Out of scope: the closed-source iPhone app (report through the same channel anyway — it will be forwarded), volumetric
DDoS, issues that need a compromised server.

You will get a reply within 7 days. Fixed issues are credited in the release notes unless you prefer otherwise.
